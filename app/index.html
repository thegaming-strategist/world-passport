How it works

Why the country in your pack is provably fair
  1. Rip: Your wallet requests randomness from the MagicBlock VRF oracle queue on Solana, with your seed mixed in.
  2. Prove: An independent oracle computes the random value with a cryptographic proof (RFC 9381). The VRF program verifies the proof on-chain. Invalid proof, no stamp.
  3. Stamp: Only then does the callback mint your country into your passport. The proof lives on-chain, so anyone can audit every rip ever made.
